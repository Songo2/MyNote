#线性代数 
# 线性组合
已知一线性无关向量组
$$
\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\dots,\alpha_{n}
$$
对其进行线性组合,生成一新向量组
$$
\begin{cases}
	\beta_{1}=a_{11}\alpha_{1}+\dots+a_{1n}\alpha_{n} \\
	\beta_{2}=a_{21}\alpha_{1}+\dots+a_{2n}\alpha_{n} \\
	\vdots \\
	\beta_{n}=a_{n1}\alpha_{1}+\dots+a_{nn}\alpha_{n}
\end{cases}
$$
判断$\beta$向量组的相关性
___
我们将其视为一线性变换,记其系数矩阵为
$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
	a_{11} & a_{12} & \dots & a_{1n} \\
	a_{21} & a_{22} & \dots & a_{2n} \\
	\vdots & \vdots &  & \vdots \\
	a_{n1} & a_{n2} & \dots & a_{nn}
\end{pmatrix}
$$

故$\beta=\alpha A^T$
若$\beta$线性相关,则$|A|=0$
若$\beta$线性无关,则$|A|\ne 0$
___

# 矩阵变换
已知A是一个方阵,有一线性无关向量组
$$
\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\dots,\alpha_{n}
$$
利用矩阵A对其进行变换,可得向量组
$$
\begin{cases}
	\beta_{1}=A\alpha_{1} \\
	\beta_{2}=A\alpha_{2} \\
	\vdots \\
	\beta_{n}=A\alpha_{n}
\end{cases}
$$
判断$\beta$向量组的相关性
___
设$$
k_{1}\beta_{1}+k_{2}\beta_{2}+\dots+k_{n}\beta_{n}=0
$$
则可变为
$$
A(k_{1}\alpha_{1}+k_{2}\alpha_{2}+\dots+k_{n}\alpha_{n})=0
$$
当A可逆时
两边同时乘$A^{-1}$可得
$$
\begin{align}
A^{-1}A(k_{1}\alpha_{1}+k_{2}\alpha_{2}+\dots+k_{n}\alpha_{n})=0A^{-1} \\
\implies k_{1}\alpha_{1}+k_{2}\alpha_{2}+\dots+k_{n}\alpha_{n}=0 \\
\implies k_{1}=k_{2}=\dots=k_{n}=0
\end{align}
$$
显然成立,故向量组$\beta$线性无关
当A不可逆,即$|A|=0$
则$Ax=0$有非零解$\xi$
$\xi=k_{1}\alpha_{1}+k_{2}\alpha_{2}+\dots+k_{n}\alpha_{n}$
则假设不成立,向量组$\beta$线性相关
___
