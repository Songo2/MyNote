#多元函数积分学 
[[8-第一类曲面积分]]

# 有向曲面

## 定义

对于曲面,其可以分为单侧和双侧曲面,我们利用曲面上法向量的指向来确定曲面的侧,确定了侧的曲面称为**有向曲面**
___
## 投影

设$\Sigma$是有向曲面,$\Delta S$为上面的一小块曲面,其在$xoy$面上的投影记为$(\Delta S)_{xy}$,其面积为$(\Delta \sigma)_{xy}$,曲面上各点处的法向量与$z$轴的夹角为$\gamma$,则
$$
(\Delta S)_{xy}=
\begin{cases}
	(\Delta \sigma)_{xy} & \cos \gamma>0 \\
	-(\Delta \sigma)_{xy} & \cos \gamma<0 \\
	0 & \cos \gamma=0
\end{cases}
$$
也即
$$
(\Delta S)_{xy}=\Delta S \cos \gamma
$$
类似的有
$$
\begin{gather}
	(\Delta S)_{yz}=
\begin{cases}
	(\Delta \sigma)_{yz} & \cos \alpha>0 \\
	-(\Delta \sigma)_{yz} & \cos \alpha<0 \\
	0 & \cos \alpha=0
\end{cases}\quad (\Delta S)_{yz}=\Delta S\cos \alpha \\ \\
(\Delta S)_{xz}=
\begin{cases}
	(\Delta \sigma)_{xz} & \cos \beta>0 \\
	-(\Delta \sigma)_{xz} & \cos \beta<0 \\
	0 & \cos \beta=0
\end{cases}\quad (\Delta S)_{xz}=\Delta S\cos \beta
\end{gather}
$$
___
# 第二类曲面积分

## 定义

设$\Sigma$为光滑或分片光滑的有向曲面,$\vec{\nu}=\{ P(x,y,z),Q(x,y,z),R(x,y,z) \}$在$\Sigma$上有定义且连续
- 将$\Sigma$任意分成$n$个小片$\Delta S_{i}$(也表示对应的面积),$\Delta S_{i}$在$xoy(或yoz,zo x)$面上的投影为$(\Delta S_{i})_{xy}(或(\Delta S_{i})_{yz},(\Delta S_{i})_{xz})$
- $\forall M_{i}(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i})\in \Delta S_{i}$,有$\vec{n}(M_{i})=\{ \cos \alpha,\cos \beta,\cos \gamma \},\vec{\nu}(M_{i})=\{ P(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i}),Q(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i}),R(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i}) \}$
取
$$
\begin{gather}
	\sum_{i=1}^{n}\vec{\nu}(M_{i})\cdot \vec{n}(M_{i})\cdot \Delta S_{i} \\
	=\sum_{i=1}^{n}[P(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i})\cos \alpha_{i},Q(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i})\cos \beta_{i},R(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i}\cos \gamma_{i})]\Delta S_{i} \\
	=\sum_{i=1}^{n}[P(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i})(\Delta S_{i})_{yz},Q(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i})(\Delta S_{i})_{zx},R(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i})(\Delta S_{i})_{xy}]
\end{gather}
$$
- 取$\lambda=max_{1 \le i \le n}\{ \Delta S_{i}的直径 \}$,若
$$
\begin{gather}
	\sum_{i=1}^{n}\vec{\nu}(M_{i})\cdot \vec{n}(M_{i})\cdot \Delta S_{i} \\
	=\sum_{i=1}^{n}[P(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i})\cos \alpha_{i},Q(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i})\cos \beta_{i},R(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i}\cos \gamma_{i})]\Delta S_{i} \\
	=\sum_{i=1}^{n}[P(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i})(\Delta S_{i})_{yz},Q(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i})(\Delta S_{i})_{zx},R(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i})(\Delta S_{i})_{xy}]
\end{gather}
$$
的存在与$M_{i}$的取法和$\Sigma$的取法无关,则称此极限为$\vec{\nu}$在$\Sigma$上的对坐标的曲面积分,也即**第二类曲面积分**,记为
$$
\begin{gather}
	\iint_{\Sigma}\vec{\nu}\cdot \vec{n}dS=\iint_{\Sigma}(P\cos \alpha+Q\cos \beta+R\cos \gamma)dS \\
	=\iint_{\Sigma}P(x,y,z)dydz+Q(x,y,z)dzdx+R(x,y,z)dxdy
\end{gather}
$$
其中
$$
\iint R(x,y,z)dxdy=\lim_{ \lambda \to 0 }\sum_{i=1}^{n}R(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i})(\Delta S_{i})_{xy} 
$$
是对坐标$x,y$的曲面积分
$$
\iint P(x,y,z)dydz=\lim_{ \lambda \to 0 }\sum_{i=1}^{n}P(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i})(\Delta S_{i})_{yz} 
$$
是对坐标$y,z$的曲面积分
$$
\iint Q(x,y,z)dzdx=\lim_{ \lambda \to 0 }\sum_{i=1}^{n}Q(\xi_{i},\eta_{i},\zeta_{i})(\Delta S_{i})_{zx} 
$$
是对坐标$x,y$的曲面积分

当$P,Q,R$在有向光滑曲面$\Sigma$上连续时,第二类曲面积分存在
___
## 性质

- 线性$$
\begin{gather}
	\iint_{\Sigma}APdydz+BQdzdx+CRdxdy \\
	=A\iint_{\Sigma}Pdydz+B\iint_{\Sigma}Qdzdx+C\iint_{\Sigma}Rdxdy
\end{gather}
$$
- 可加性$$
\begin{gather}
	\iint_{\Sigma}Pdydz+Qdzdx+Rdxdy \\
	=\iint_{\Sigma_{1}}Pdydz+Qdzdx+Rdxdy+\iint_{\Sigma_{2}}Pdydz+Qdzdx+Rdxdy \\
	(\Sigma=\Sigma_{1}+\Sigma_{2})
\end{gather}
$$
- 方向性$$
\iint_{\Sigma}Pdydz+Qdzdx+Rdxdy=-\iint_{-\Sigma}PDydz+Qdzdx+Rdxdy
$$
___
## 计算求解

计算$\iint_{\Sigma}R(x,y,z)dxdy$
取$\Sigma$的方程:$z=z(x,y),(x,y)\in D_{xy}$则
当$\Sigma$取上侧时
$$
\iint_{\Sigma}R(x,y,z)dxdy=\iint_{D_{xy}}R(x,y,z(x,y))dxdy
$$
当$\Sigma$取下侧时
$$
\iint_{\Sigma}R(x,y,z)dxdy=-\iint_{D_{xy}}R(x,y,z(x,y))dxdy
$$
类似的有

计算$\iint_{\Sigma}P(x,y,z)dydz$
取$\Sigma$的方程:$x=x(y,z),(y,z)\in D_{yz}$则
当$\Sigma$取前侧时
$$
\iint_{\Sigma}P(x,y,z)dydz=\iint_{D_{yz}}P(x(y,z),y,z)dydz
$$
当$\Sigma$取后侧时
$$
\iint_{\Sigma}P(x,y,z)dydz=-\iint_{D_{yz}}P(x(y,z),y,z)dydz
$$
计算$\iint_{\Sigma}Q(x,y,z)dzdx$
取$\Sigma$的方程:$y=y(z,x),(z,x)\in D_{zx}$则
当$\Sigma$取左侧时
$$
\iint_{\Sigma}Q(x,y,z)dzdx=\iint_{D_{zx}}Q(x,y(z,x),z)dzdx
$$
当$\Sigma$取右侧时
$$
\iint_{\Sigma}Q(x,y,z)dzdx=-\iint_{D_{zx}}Q(x,y(z,x),z)dzdx
$$
___
## 对称化简

$\Sigma$关于$xoy$面对称
$$
\begin{gather}
	\iint_{\Sigma}R(x,y,z)dxdy=\begin{cases}
		2\int_{\Sigma_{上}}R(x,y,z)dxdy & R(x,y,-z)=R(x,y,z) \\
		0 & R(x,y,-z)=-R(x,y,z)
	\end{cases}
\end{gather}
$$

$\Sigma$关于$yoz$面对称
$$
\begin{gather}
	\iint_{\Sigma}P(x,y,z)dydz=\begin{cases}
		2\int_{\Sigma_{前}}P(x,y,z)dydz & P(-x,y,z)=P(x,y,z) \\
		0 & P(-x,y,z)=-P(x,y,z)
	\end{cases}
\end{gather}
$$

$\Sigma$关于$xoz$面对称
$$
\begin{gather}
	\iint_{\Sigma}Q(x,y,z)dzdx=\begin{cases}
		2\int_{\Sigma_{左}}Q(x,y,z)dzdx & Q(x,-y,z)=Q(x,y,z) \\
		0 & Q(x,-y,z)=-Q(x,y,z)
	\end{cases}
\end{gather}
$$
