# 多元复合函数的微分

对于多元函数的复合函数,外层函数的是多元函数,内层函数也是多元函数

对于多元函数,我们知道它的导数已经变化为多个偏导数

而一元函数根据链式法则可以展开出复合函数的导数

多元函数的链式法则是怎么样的呢
___
## 链式法则的推广

显然,各个偏导数是用了一元函数的导数的概念推广而来,故单独的偏导数是符合链式法则的

例如$z=f(u,v),u=u(x,y),v=v(x,y)$
$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}?=\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}
$$
然而这就足够了吗
显然,$v$也有对$x$的偏导数,故而应该还有一部分
$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial x}
$$

故而我们可以发现,要经过外层函数去对内层变量求偏导,需要遍历所有含这个变量的偏导数

即链式法则推广为"网状法则"或"树状法则"
___
## 多元复合微分定理

我们有定理
若$u=u(x,y),v=v(x,y)$在点$(x,y)$可偏导,$z=f(u,v)$在相应点$(u,v)$有连续偏导数,则$z=f(u(x,y),v(x,y))$在$(x,y)$可偏导,有
$$
\begin{gather}
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial x} \\
\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial y}	
\end{gather}
$$
我们写成向量形式有
$$
\begin{gather}
	\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=\begin{pmatrix}
		f_{u} & f_{v}
	\end{pmatrix}\cdot \begin{pmatrix}
		u_{x} \\
		v_{x}
	\end{pmatrix} \\
	\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=\begin{pmatrix}
		f_{u} & f_{v}
	\end{pmatrix}\cdot \begin{pmatrix}
		u_{y} \\
		v_{y}
	\end{pmatrix}
\end{gather}
$$
___
- 如果$z=f(u,v)$仅有偏导而不连续,则定理失效
- 如果$z=f(u,v)$有连续偏导数,则$u,v$的条件可以减弱为可微
- 对自变量求偏导数时,需要经过所有可能的中间变量,再回归到最终的变量

$$
需要注意的是,我们在式子中可以看到两个项,一个是$\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}$,另一个是$\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}$,这两个在之前我们不复合的单层函数里认为是同一个量的不同记法,在这里怎么出现了分歧呢

实际上这是两个不同的结果,由于不同层出现了相同的变量导致的,其中$\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}$是根据第三层函数展开的$z(x,y)$的偏导数,而$\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}$是单单从第二层函数展开的$z=(u,x,y)$的偏导数

	\end{pmatrix} 
	\cdot
	\begin{pmatrix}
		\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} \\
		1 \\
		0
	\end{pmatrix} \\
	\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=\begin{pmatrix}
		\frac{\partial f}{\partial u} & \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}
	\end{pmatrix}
	\cdot
	\begin{pmatrix}
		\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} \\
		0 \\
		1
	\end{pmatrix}
\end{gather}
$$

	 \\

## 多元复合函数的高阶偏导数

设$u=\varphi(x,y),v=\psi(x,y)$有二阶连续偏导,$z=f(u,v)$在$(u,v)$有二阶连续偏导,则复合函数$z=f[\varphi(x,y),\psi(x,y)]$在$(x,y)$处有二阶偏导
___
### 计算举例

#### 举例1

$$
\begin{gather}
	u=f(x+y+z,xyz)
\end{gather}
$$
$f$具有二阶连续偏导数,求
$$
\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x \partial z}
$$
___
不妨设$v=x+y+z,w=xyz$
树状图如下
```mermaid

D1[x]
D2[y]
D3[z]
end
A-->B1 & B2
B1-->C1 & C2 & C3
B2-->D1 & D2 & D3
```
先求$\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}$
$$
\begin{gather}
	\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=\frac{\partial f}{\partial u}\cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial f}{\partial v}\cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial x} \\
	=f_{1}'+yzf_{2}'
\end{gather}
$$
再求$\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x\partial z}$
$$
\begin{gather}
	\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x\partial z}=\frac{\partial f_{1}'}{\partial u}\cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial z}+\frac{\partial f_{1}'}{\partial v}\cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial z} +yf_{2}'+yz \left( \frac{\partial f_{2}'}{\partial u} \frac{\partial u}{\partial z}+\frac{\partial f_{2}'}{\partial v}\cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial z}  \right) \\
	=f_{11}''+xyf_{12}''+yf_{2}'+yzf_{21}''+xy^2zf_{22}'' \\
	=f_{11}''+y(x+z)f_{12}''+xy^2zf_{22}'+yf_{2}'
\end{gather}
$$

#### 举例3

$u=f(x,y)$有二阶连续导数,将$\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}, \frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}$换为极坐标形式
___
$$

	r=\sqrt{ x^2+y^2 }, \theta=\arctan \frac{y}{x} \\
	设u=u(r,\theta)=u\left( \sqrt{ x^2+y^2 }, \arctan \frac{y}{x}  \right)=f(x,y) \\
	\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=\frac{\partial u}{\partial r}\cdot \frac{\partial r}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial u}{\partial \theta}\cdot \frac{\partial \theta}{\partial x}
\end{gather}
$$
树状图如下
```mermaid

end

```
我们来计算一下其中的$r,\theta$的偏导
$$
\begin{gather}
	\frac{\partial r}{\partial x}=\frac{x}{\sqrt{ x^2+y^2 }}=\frac{x}{r} \\
	\frac{\partial r}{\partial y}=\frac{y}{\sqrt{ x^2+y^2 }}=\frac{y}{r} \\
	\frac{\partial \theta}{\partial x}=\frac{ -\frac{y}{x^2} }{1+\left( \frac{y}{x} \right)^2}=-\frac{y}{x^2+y^2}=-\frac{y}{r^2} \\
	\frac{\partial \theta}{\partial y}=\frac{ \frac{1}{x} }{1+\left( \frac{y}{x} \right)^2}=\frac{x}{\sqrt{ x^2+y^2 }}=\frac{x}{r^2}
\end{gather}
$$
我们再改$x,y$成$r\cos \theta,r\cos \theta$
$$
\begin{gather}
	\frac{\partial r}{\partial x}=\cos \theta \\
	\frac{\partial r}{\partial y}=\sin \theta \\
	\frac{\partial \theta}{\partial x}=-\frac{\sin \theta}{r} \\
	\frac{\partial \theta}{\partial y}=\frac{\cos \theta}{r}
\end{gather}
$$
___
故我们先求一阶偏导
$$
\begin{gather}
	\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=\frac{\partial u}{\partial r}\cdot \frac{\partial r}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial u}{\partial \theta}\cdot \frac{\partial \theta}{\partial x} \\
	=\frac{\partial u}{\partial r}\cos \theta-\frac{\partial u}{\partial \theta}\cdot \frac{\sin \theta}{r} \\ \\
	\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial u}{\partial r}\cdot \frac{\partial r}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial u}{\partial \theta}\cdot \frac{\partial \theta}{\partial y} \\
	=\frac{\partial u}{\partial r}\sin \theta+\frac{\partial u}{\partial \theta}\cdot \frac{\cos \theta}{y}
\end{gather}
$$
再求二阶偏导
$$
\begin{gather}
	\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}=\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\left( \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} \right)\cdot \frac{\partial r}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta}\left( \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} \right)\cdot \frac{\partial \theta}{\partial x} \\
	=\left( \frac{\partial^2u}{\partial r^2}\cos \theta-\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial \theta \partial r}\cdot \frac{\sin \theta}{r}+\frac{\partial u}{\partial \theta}\cdot \frac{\sin \theta}{r^2} \right)\cos \theta \\
	+\left( \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial r \partial \theta}\cos \theta-\frac{\partial u}{\partial r}\sin \theta-\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial \theta^2}\cdot \frac{\sin \theta}{r}-\frac{\partial u}{\partial \theta}\cdot \frac{\cos \theta}{r} \right)\left( -\frac{\sin \theta}{r} \right) \\ \\
	\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}=\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\left( \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} \right)\cdot \frac{\partial r}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta}\left( \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} \right)\cdot \frac{\partial \theta}{\partial y} \\
	=\left( \frac{\partial^2u}{\partial r^2}\sin \theta+\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial \theta \partial r}\cdot \frac{\cos \theta}{r}-\frac{\partial u}{\partial \theta}\cdot \frac{\cos \theta}{r^2} \right)\sin \theta \\
	+\left( \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial r \partial \theta}\sin \theta+ \frac{\partial u}{\partial r}\cos \theta +\frac{\partial^2}{\partial \theta^2}\cdot \frac{\cos \theta}{r}-\frac{\partial u}{\partial \theta}\cdot \frac{\cos \theta}{r} \right)\cdot \frac{\cos \theta}{r}
\end{gather}
$$
___
对此,其实有
$$
\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}=\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial r^2}+\frac{1}{r}\cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial r}+\frac{1}{r^2}\cdot \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial \theta^2} 
$$

# 一阶微分形式不变性

设函数$z=f(u,v)$具有连续偏导数,则有全微分
$$
dz=\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}du+\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}dv
$$
又若$u,v$是$x,y$的函数,即$u=\varphi(x,y),v=\psi(x,y)$且有连续偏导,则$z=f[\varphi(x,y),\psi(x,y)]$的全微分为
$$
dz=\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}dx+\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}dy
$$
此时,如果我们将层次倒转,从$x,y$导向$u,v$,可得
$$
\begin{gather}
	dz=\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}dx+\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}dy \\
	=\left( \frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial x} \right)dx+\left( \frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial u}{\partial v}\cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} \right)dy \\
	我们将其重新组合 \\
	=\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\left( \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}dx+\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}dy \right)+\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\left( \frac{\partial v}{\partial x}dx+\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}dy \right) \\
	利用全微分,我们可得 \\
	=\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}du+\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}dv
\end{gather}
$$
可见
$$
\begin{gather}
	dz \\
	=\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}dx+\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}dy \\
	=\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}du+\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}dv
\end{gather}
$$
我们称之为**一阶微分形式不变性**,无论$u,v$是以中间变量还是最终变量进行微分,都能得到
$$
dz=\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}du+\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}dv
$$
## 计算举例

### 举例1

$$
\begin{gather}
	z=e^u \sin v \\
	u=xy,v=x+y \\
	求 \frac{\partial z}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}
\end{gather}
$$
___
$$
\begin{gather}
	dz=d(e^u\sin v) \\
	=e^u \sin v du+e^u\cos v dv \\
	我们用u,v的全微分展开 \\
	du=dx+dy \\
	dv=ydx+xdy \\ \\
	dz=e^u \sin v(dx+dy)+e^u\cos v(ydx+xdy) \\
	=(e^u\sin v+ye^u\cos v)dx+(e^u\sin v+xe^u\cos v)dy \\
	=e^{xy}[y\sin(x+y)+\cos(x+y)]dx+e^{xy}[x\sin (x+y)+\cos(x+y)]dy \\ \\
	可得 \\
	\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=e^{xy}[y\sin(x+y)+\cos(x+y)] \\
	\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=e^{xy}[x\sin(x+y)+\cos(x+y)]
\end{gather}
$$
___
